## Slide 2 - Problem (0:45)

**Say this:**
- "In Bab Berred, Moroccan mountains, there is NOT enough water"
- "Rain is declining every year"
- "Farmers need water for farming"
- "What do they do? They build basins"

**Say this:**

- "The real reason = money from cannabis"
- "Old cannabis makes 15 kg per hectare"

- "That is 4-5 times more!"

**Say this:**
- "How did we study this?"
- "We use satellite pictures - Landsat satellite"
- "We count basins by looking at pictures - 1998 to 2025"
- "We also measure rain - 1985 to 2025"
- "We measure green vegetation using NDVI"

- "We use computer programs: QGIS, Google Earth Engine, RStudio"

## Slide 6 - Basin Numbers (1:00)

- "In 2010 there were about 28 basins"
- "In 2025 there are 2,044 basins"
- "That is 73 times more!"
- "In just 15 years!"
- "Also - water storage:"

- "That is 85 times more water!"

## Slide 8 - Vegetation Greens Up (0:50)

**Say this:**
- "We measure vegetation from space using NDVI"
- "NDVI = how green the land is"
- "Before 2012: Slowly getting greener"
- "After 2012: Very green! Big jump!"
- "Most green in summer when cannabis is big"
- "Show the graph - see the increase after 2012"

**Simple message**: Vegetation is very green, especially since 2012

## Slide 9 - But Rain Declines! (0:50)

**Say this:**
- "Wait! There is a problem"
- "Look at rain - it is declining"
- "Long-term: Less and less rain"
- "2023 = driest year ever (only 300 mm!)"
- "Statistical test shows: Trend is DOWN"
- "Show the graph - see it goes down"

**Simple message**: Rain is declining, but vegetation is getting greener - WHY?

## Slide 10 - The Paradox Explained (1:00)

**Say this:**
- "This is strange - vegetation UP but rain DOWN"
- "How is this possible?"
- "ANSWER: Irrigation!"
- "Basins store water from rain"
- "Farmers water cannabis with stored water"
- "Cannabis gets water $\Rightarrow$ green"
- "Satellites see green $\Rightarrow$ high NDVI"
- "But is this good? No!"
- "Natural forest = gone"
- "Only cannabis fields = green"
- "So high NDVI is FAKE improvement"
- "It is just farming, not nature"

**Simple message**: Water from basins makes vegetation green, but it hides land conversion

**Say this:**

- "Also - ecosystem problem:"
- "Natural forest = disappearing"
- "Basins and farms = everywhere"
- "Endemic species = at risk"
- "So - basins solve short-term problem"
- "But create long-term problem"

**Simple message**: Current solution will cause future crisis

## 💡 Easy Tips for Presenting

1. **Talk slowly** - English may be hard for some
2. **Point at graphs** - Don't just show, explain
3. **Repeat numbers** - "2010: 28 basins. 2025: 2,044 basins"
4. **Short sentences** - Don't use long, complex sentences
5. **Use the word "simple"** - "Simple answer:", "Simple problem:"
6. **Pause after key points** - Give people time to understand
7. **Don't apologize** for accent - Just speak clearly

## ⏱️ Timing

- Slide 1-4: 3 min (introduction)
- Slide 5: 1 min (methods)
- Slide 6-9: 3.5 min (results - 4 slides)
- Slide 10-11: 2 min (discussion - 2 slides)
- Slide 12: 0.5 min (conclusion)
- **Total = 10 minutes exactly**
